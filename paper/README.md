Core contribution: a controlled system-level study of how active cooling changes sustained native BitNet inference performance, thermal behaviour, CPU frequency stability, and whole-system energy consumption on a Raspberry Pi 5.

Before submission, this section should include the paper manuscript, final figures/tables, bibliography, and any additional repeated energy or baseline experiments used for publication.
